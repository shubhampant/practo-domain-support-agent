# Cancellation and Rescheduling Window

These are fictional capstone rules, not official Practo cancellation policies. In this simulation, a user may request cancellation or rescheduling at least 24 hours before the appointment start time without a simulated late-change restriction. Requests made less than 24 hours before the appointment require human-support review, and the agent must not promise approval or a refund. Completed and already-cancelled appointments cannot be rescheduled through this workflow, and every change must be verified against the appointment record.
