# Follow-up Visit Discount Policy

These are synthetic capstone rules, not official Practo discount terms. For this simulation, a follow-up discount may be considered only when the appointment record indicates that follow-up is required and the request is within 30 days of the original appointment creation date. The simulated discount is 10 percent of the recorded consultation fee, subject to human review before it is confirmed or applied. If the eligibility data is missing, contradictory, or outside these conditions, the agent must not promise a discount.
