# Prescription Refill Policy

These are fictional capstone support rules, not official Practo medical policies. A refill request must be directed to the prescribing clinician for review and authorization, and the support agent must not prescribe medicine or independently change a dose. The agent may explain the administrative steps for contacting the clinician but must not guarantee that a refill will be approved. For a possible serious adverse reaction or urgent medical concern, the user should seek prompt professional care or emergency assistance as appropriate.
